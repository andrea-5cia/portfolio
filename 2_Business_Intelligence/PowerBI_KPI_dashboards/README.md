# 📊 Degree Programs Self-Assessment KPI Dashboards (Power BI)

## 🎯 Objective
Design and deliver **Power BI dashboards** to support the **annual self-assessment (autovalutazione)** of university degree programs, in line with **AVA3 Quality Assurance requirements**.

The dashboards provide a **standardized, data-driven overview** of student careers, academic performance, and key QA indicators, supporting Course Boards and Quality Committees in evidence-based evaluation.

---

## 🧰 Tools
- **Power BI Desktop**
- Institutional administrative data (student careers, exams, graduates)
- AVA3 official indicators and benchmarks

---

## 🎓 Scope
Two separate reports were developed:
- **Bachelor’s Degree Programs (Laurea Triennale)**
- **Master’s Degree Programs (Laurea Magistrale)**

Each report follows the **same analytical structure**, ensuring comparability and reuse across degree levels.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Content

### 1. Exams & Academic Performance
- Distribution of exam grades by academic year and year of study  
- Average grades and number of passed exams  
- Share of honors (*lode*)  
- Regularity of exam completion within the expected academic year  
  *(see tables and distributions on pages 1–2 of each report)*

---

### 2. Graduates Analysis
- Number of graduates per year  
- Average and median graduation grade  
- Standard deviation of final grades  
- Share of students graduating on time  
- Relationship between entry grade, final grade, and regularity  
  *(visualized in histograms and scatter plots, page 3)*

---

### 3. AVA3 Indicators & Benchmarks
- Core **AVA3 – Ambito F indicators** (F.0.0.A – F.0.0.I)
- Trend analysis over multiple academic years  
- Comparison against:
  - Area geografica benchmark  
  - National benchmark  
- Dedicated KPI trend dashboards with interactive filtering  
  *(see pages 4 and 7)* 

---

### 4. Incoming Students Profile
- Geographic origin of enrolled students (Italy & abroad)
- Interactive maps and regional distributions  
- University of origin (Top N feeder institutions)
- Citizenship analysis for international students  
  *(pages 5–6)*

---

## 🧠 Design Principles
- **One report = one degree program**
- Fully **parameterized structure**, easily replicable for other programs
- Visual consistency across Bachelor’s and Master’s dashboards
- Focus on **interpretability**, not just visualization
- Alignment with official QA language and indicators

---

## 🧩 Use Cases
- Annual **Self-Assessment Reports**
- Support to **Course Boards (CdS)**
- Evidence base for **Quality Committees and CPDS**
- Preparation of documentation for accreditation and monitoring
